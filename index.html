About NDti & IanArt

Inspire your space through art and design.

At NDti, we are dedicated to the art of creating custom bespoke joinery that transcends the ordinary. With a passion for craftsmanship and an eye for design, we collaborate with the talented artist IanArt to turn spaces into stunning masterpieces. From kitchens to bedroom cupboards, bathroom vanities to studies and offices, our skilled team meticulously crafts joinery pieces that are not only functional but also works of art.

We believe in the power of collaboration and work closely with our clients and interior designers to bring their visions to life. Let us transform your space into a beautiful place that inspires.

Services & Scope

What bespoke joinery and interior services do you offer?

We design and manufacture bespoke joinery solutions for designer kitchens, bedroom cupboards, luxury bathroom vanities, living room TV units & acoustic slat walls, studies & home offices, wine cellars, custom bars, and turnkey commercial office fitouts.
3D Visualizations

How accurate are your 3D concept renders to the finished product?

Our 3D renderings are millimetre-precise architectural models. As seen in our Portfolio 3D Render vs Finished Reality comparison showcase on this page, our finished joinery matches the approved 3D concept with flawless precision in materials, lighting, and finishes.
Collaboration

Do you work alongside our architect or interior designer?

Absolutely! Collaboration is at the core of what we do. We routinely partner with interior designers, architects, and homeowners across Cape Town and the Western Cape to turn design concepts into stunning reality.
Locations & Workshop

Where are your workshops located and what areas do you service?

Our workshop and office are situated at Unit 2, 15 Muscat Road, Saxenburg Park 1, Blackheath. We service the greater Cape Town area, the Atlantic Seaboard, Southern Suburbs, Winelands, and select national projects.
Materials & Quality

What materials, substrates, and hardware guarantees do you provide?

We use moisture-resistant HMR and marine-grade substrates, natural veneers, quartz and sintered stone surfaces, and industry-leading German Blum concealed soft-close runners and hinges backed by comprehensive workmanship guarantees.
Consultations

How do I request a quote or design consultation?

You can reach out via our contact form, email us directly at info@ianartndti.co.za, call 083 800 8834, or click our WhatsApp button to discuss your project directly with our master craftsmen!
