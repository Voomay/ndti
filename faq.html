Clear answers about our custom bespoke joinery, design process, materials, and turnkey fitting.

  • Design & 3D Renderings
  • Materials & Hardware
  • Process, Timelines & Fitting
  • What bespoke joinery services do NDti & IanArt provide?
    We design and manufacture custom joinery solutions across all architectural spaces: Designer Kitchens, Master Bedroom Cupboards & Walk-In Closets, Luxury Bathroom Vanities, Living Room TV Units & Feature Walls, Studies & Executive Home Offices, Wine Cellars, and Corporate Commercial Joinery.
    How does your 3D Render to Reality process work?
    We begin with in-depth spatial measurements and understanding your lifestyle needs. Our designers construct photorealistic 3D architectural renders showing exact materials, proportions, lighting, and finishes. Once approved, our workshop crafts each piece to match the render with millimetre precision.
    Can you collaborate with our architect or interior designer?
    Yes! Close collaboration is at the very core of what we do. We routinely partner with homeowners, independent interior designers, and architects to translate creative concepts into seamless joinery masterpieces.
    What is the difference between custom bespoke joinery and modular cabinetry?
    Modular cabinetry consists of mass-produced, standardized box sizes requiring wide filler panels to fit non-standard spaces. NDti bespoke joinery is made-to-measure down to the millimetre, maximizing storage, matching room contours, and incorporating unique architectural details that modular systems cannot achieve.
  • What hardware brands and mechanisms do you use?
    We exclusively specify premium European hardware systems, such as Blum and Grass. This includes soft-close concealed hinges, motorized or tip-on touch-to-open drawer runners, and heavy-duty pantry pull-outs engineered for lifetime durability.
    How do you protect cabinetry in bathrooms and kitchens against moisture?
    We utilize High Moisture Resistance (HMR) substrates and marine-grade boards. Furthermore, our edges are sealed with PUR (polyurethane) hot-melt adhesive edge-banding, which forms a waterproof barrier that prevents steam and water ingress from causing swelling.
    What finishes and surface treatments are available?
    We offer an extensive palette of luxury finishes: ultra-matte anti-fingerprint surfaces, high-gloss lacquers, genuine natural timber veneers (Oak, Walnut, Ash), fluted acoustic paneling, and custom hand-finished artistic textures in collaboration with IanArt.
    How does the artistic collaboration with IanArt work?
    Our exclusive partnership with IanArt Pty Ltd allows us to incorporate custom artistic design elements into cabinetry, such as decorative screening, bespoke patina metals, hand-applied textures, and artistic handles that elevate joinery into functional fine art.
  • What is the typical project timeline from consultation to installation?
    Initial consultation and 3D rendering typically take 1 to 2 weeks. Precision manufacturing in our Blackheath facility takes 3 to 5 weeks. On-site installation by our in-house fitting team usually takes 3 to 7 days depending on the size of the project.
    Where is NDti located and which areas do you service?
    Our workshop and studio are located at Unit 2, 15 Muscat Road, Saxenburg Park 1, Blackheath, Cape Town. We service the Cape Peninsula, Southern Suburbs, Atlantic Seaboard, Northern Suburbs, Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, and broader Western Cape.
    Do you provide aftercare and warranties on your installations?
    Yes! All NDti joinery installations are backed by comprehensive craftsmanship guarantees, alongside manufacturer warranties on all Blum and Grass hardware mechanisms. We also provide full care and maintenance guides.
    How do I request a quote or book an initial design consultation?
    Getting started is easy! You can call us directly at 083 800 8834, email info@ianartndti.co.za, submit your drawings through our contact form, or message us instantly on WhatsApp.
