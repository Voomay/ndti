Our Story

Bringing Ideas to Life with NDti and IanArt

At NDti, we are passionate about creating custom bespoke joinery that transforms spaces into stunning works of art. Collaborating with the talented artist IanArt, we bring a unique blend of craftsmanship and creativity to every project we undertake.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to bring our clients’ ideas to life. We believe in the power of close collaboration, working hand in hand with our clients and interior designers to ensure that every detail is meticulously crafted to their vision.

From kitchens to bedroom cupboards, bathroom vanities to studies and offices, our team of skilled artisans designs and manufactures bespoke joinery pieces that are not only functional but also reflect the individual style and personality of our clients.

Our Mission

We are committed to creating exceptional joinery pieces that enhance the beauty and functionality of spaces. By collaborating closely with our clients and interior designers, we aim to deliver personalized solutions that reflect individual styles, exceed expectations, and create spaces that inspire.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading provider of custom bespoke joinery, recognized for our craftsmanship, innovation, and customer-centric approach. We aspire to be the go-to choice for clients seeking unique and stunning joinery solutions, continually pushing the boundaries of architectural woodworking and fine art.
